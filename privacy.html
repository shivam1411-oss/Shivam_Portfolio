Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy applies to Shivam Soni's portfolio website (this site) and the accompanying iOS app (together, "the App"). It explains what information is collected, how it's used, and who it's shared with. This is a personal portfolio, not a commercial product, and the App collects the minimum information needed to provide the features described below.

Information We Collect

The App collects information only when you choose to use the "Get Resume" feature:

  • Name — used to personalize the email you receive.
  • Email address — used solely to send you a copy of the resume.

No other personal information is collected. The App does not use cookies, does not track your browsing activity, and does not collect location data, device identifiers, or analytics of any kind.

How We Use Your Information

The name and email you provide are used for exactly one purpose: sending you the requested resume by email. This information is not used for marketing, is not added to any mailing list, and is not used to contact you for any other reason.

Third-Party Services

To deliver the resume by email, the App sends your name and email address to EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, which processes the request and sends the message. EmailJS's own handling of this data is governed by their privacy policy, available at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

No other third parties receive your information. The App does not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage & Retention

Shivam Soni does not maintain a database of names or email addresses submitted through this feature. Your information is transmitted directly to EmailJS for the purpose of sending the email and is not separately stored, logged, or retained by this website or app beyond what EmailJS itself retains as part of its standard email delivery logs.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

If you've submitted your name and email through the "Get Resume" feature and would like to know what information was sent or would like it removed from EmailJS's delivery logs, contact us using the details below and we'll assist or direct you to EmailJS's own data request process.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the App's features. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
